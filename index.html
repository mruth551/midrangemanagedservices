Complimentary Preliminary IBM i System Review

Not sure how healthy your IBM i environment is?

Midrange Managed Services offers a complimentary preliminary review of your IBM i environment to help identify potential concerns, operational risks, and opportunities for improvement.

Areas of Review Include:

  • IBM i operating system version and PTF status
  • System storage capacity and utilization
  • Security configuration and user access controls
  • Backup procedures and disaster recovery readiness
  • High availability and replication configuration
  • System performance and operational concerns

Following the review, you'll receive a summary of observations and recommendations for consideration.

The preliminary review is not a formal security audit, penetration test, or certification.
